### TASK-036 — Design reminder-reduction experiment · status: pending → in_review

A pre-registration is, by definition, a protocol written *before* any data exists; it requires **no** participant, result, or empirical record.   - **Framing:** a **non-inferiority** design (§1.2) — the graduation thesis is "removing outgrown scaffolding does *no meaningful harm* and restores autonomy," which is a non-inferiority question, not superiority. Primary hypothesis H1 + secondary superiority (autonomy, H2) and non-inferiority (sustained SRBAI automaticity, H3) + a safety harm-screen (H4).

Blinding handled **honestly**: open-label on the manipulated variable, but blinded *objective* primary outcome (computed from the TASK-026 ledger, not judgement), attention-matched control, and arm-masked analysis. Unit = habit; multiple habits per user clustered to one arm with a pre-registered clustering adjustment.

Safety and the non-negotiable gates override all experimental/commercial concerns.
